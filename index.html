About me

I'm Faith Ariwodo, the designer behind Omega Visual. Based in Kaduna, Nigeria, I work remotely with brands and businesses around the world. Since 2023 I've created logos, brand identities, flyers, posters, and roll-up banners that help brands look professional and stand out.

I work closely with clients to shape their brand identity, build clean and consistent layouts, manage revisions, and deliver on deadlines. I'm also studying for a bachelor's degree in cyber security at Federal University Dutse.
